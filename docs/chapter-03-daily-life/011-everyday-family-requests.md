# Thinking Pattern 011 — Everyday Family Requests

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Family conversations are filled with short requests that help daily life run smoothly.

Common Hindi thoughts include:

- ज़रा पानी देना।
- दरवाज़ा बंद कर दो।
- लाइट बंद कर देना।
- पंखा चला दो।
- टीवी की आवाज़ कम कर दो।
- मेरा फोन देना।
- एक मिनट सुनना।
- ज़रा मदद कर दो।
- ये ऊपर रख दो।
- बच्चों को बुला दो।
- खाना लगा दो।
- जल्दी आ जाना।
- बाहर से आते समय दूध ले आना.
- कूड़ा बाहर रख देना.
- भूलना मत।

**Core intention**

Ask family members to do everyday tasks politely and naturally.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Could you get me a glass of water? | Neutral | Asking for water |
| Would you mind closing the door? | Polite | Door or window |
| Could you turn on the fan? | Neutral | Home utilities |
| Can you help me with this? | Neutral | Asking for help |
| Don't forget to buy some milk. | Neutral | Reminder |
| Could you call the kids? | Neutral | Family coordination |
| Please keep this upstairs. | Neutral | Household task |

---

## Nuance

Native English speakers often soften requests with **could**, **would you mind**, or **can you** instead of giving direct commands.

Compare:

- Close the door. (Direct)
- Could you close the door, please? (Natural and polite)

Among close family, shorter requests are common, but polite wording still sounds warm.

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ Give me water.

✅ Could you get me a glass of water?

**Why?** A polite request sounds more natural.

---

❌ Close the light.

✅ Turn off the light.

**Why?** We turn lights on or off; we don't "close" them.

---

❌ Bring milk while coming.

✅ Pick up some milk on your way home.

**Why?** This is the natural English pattern.

## Similar Expressions

|---|---|

| Would you mind...? | More polite requests |
| Don't forget to... | Reminding someone |

---

## Memory Tip

When asking family members for help, think **polite request**, not **command**. Small words like *could* and *please* make everyday English sound much more natural.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** Could you help me with this?
- **Safest:** Could you...?
- **Avoid:** Literal commands like "Close the light."
- **Key takeaway:** Use polite request patterns for everyday family conversations.
