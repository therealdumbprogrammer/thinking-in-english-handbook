# Thinking Pattern 024 — Local Services (Tailor, Barber, Courier & Repair Shops)

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Many everyday errands involve interacting with local service providers.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- कपड़े सिलवाने हैं।
- बाल कटवाने हैं।
- पार्सल भेजना है।
- जूते ठीक कराने हैं।
- मोबाइल रिपेयर करना है।
- ये कब तक तैयार होगा?
- आज मिल जाएगा?
- कितने पैसे लगेंगे?
- जल्दी हो सकता है?
- रसीद दे दीजिए।
- घर पहुँचा देंगे?
- अभी तैयार नहीं हुआ?
- थोड़ा इंतज़ार कर लूँ?
- कल आकर ले जाऊँगा।
- धन्यवाद।

**Core intention**

Ask about everyday services, timelines, pricing and collection in a polite, natural way.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| I'd like to get this repaired. | Neutral | Repair shops |
| I'd like a haircut. | Neutral | Barber |
| I'd like to send this parcel. | Neutral | Courier |
| When will it be ready? | Neutral | Collection |
| How much will it cost? | Neutral | Price enquiry |
| I'll pick it up tomorrow. | Neutral | Collecting items |

---

## Nuance

English conversations at local service shops are usually direct and polite. State what you need first, then ask about the price or completion time.

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ Do my haircut.

✅ I'd like a haircut.

**Why?** Native speakers request the service rather than describing the action.

---

❌ Repair my mobile.

✅ I'd like to get my phone repaired.

**Why?** This sounds more natural and polite.

---

❌ When ready?

✅ When will it be ready?

**Why?** Use a complete question.

## Indian English vs Natural English

|---|---|

| Repair my mobile. | I'd like to get my phone repaired. |

## Memory Tip

Think in terms of **requesting a service**, not giving instructions. English naturally begins with *I'd like...* or *Could you...?*

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** I'd like to get this repaired.
- **Safest:** When will it be ready?
- **Avoid:** "Do my haircut."
- **Key takeaway:** State the service you need, then ask about the time or cost.
