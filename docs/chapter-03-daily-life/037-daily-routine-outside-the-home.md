# Thinking Pattern 037 — Daily Routine Outside the Home

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Many everyday thoughts happen between leaving home and reaching your destination.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- चलो निकलते हैं।
- रास्ते में हूँ।
- ट्रैफिक बहुत है।
- पाँच मिनट में पहुँचता हूँ।
- रास्ते में कुछ ले लेंगे।
- आज सड़क बहुत खाली है।
- जल्दी निकलना चाहिए था।
- अभी सिग्नल पर हूँ।
- पैदल चलते हैं।
- ऑटो मिल जाएगा।
- यहीं इंतज़ार करते हैं।
- थोड़ा जल्दी चलो।
- देर हो जाएगी।
- पहुँचने वाले हैं।
- आ गया।

**Core intention**

Talk naturally about routine movement outside the home, including delays, location updates, and simple travel decisions.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| I'm on my way. | Neutral | Traveling to a destination |
| I'll be there in five minutes. | Neutral | Giving an ETA |
| There's a lot of traffic. | Neutral | Explaining a delay |
| Let's walk. | Casual | Short distance |
| We're almost there. | Casual | Near the destination |
| I've just arrived. | Neutral | Arrival |

---

## Nuance

Native speakers usually describe **their current situation** rather than narrating every step.

Examples:

- I'm on my way.
- I'm stuck in traffic.
- I'll be there soon.

These expressions are concise and common in daily conversation.

## Common Mistakes

❌ I'm coming in five minutes.

✅ I'll be there in five minutes.

**Why?** English usually focuses on arrival rather than movement.

---

❌ Traffic is there.

✅ There's a lot of traffic.

**Why?** This is the natural expression.

---

❌ Reached.

✅ I've just arrived.

**Why?** Use a complete sentence.

---

## Indian English vs Natural English

| Common Indian English | Natural English |
|---|---|
| I'm coming in five minutes. | I'll be there in five minutes. |
| Traffic is there. | There's a lot of traffic. |
| Reached. | I've just arrived. |

## Memory Tip

When you're outside, English usually answers one question: **Where are you right now?** Describe your current situation rather than translating your movement.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** I'm on my way.
- **Safest:** I'll be there in five minutes.
- **Avoid:** "I'm coming in five minutes."
- **Key takeaway:** Give clear, natural location updates instead of translating Hindi literally.
