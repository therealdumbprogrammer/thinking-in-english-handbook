# Thinking Pattern 039 — Children & Everyday Home Conversations

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Parents speak to children dozens of times every day. These conversations are short, repetitive, and full of gentle instructions, reminders, encouragement, and reassurance.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- जल्दी तैयार हो जाओ।
- स्कूल का बैग ले लो।
- जूते पहन लो।
- हाथ धो लो।
- खाना खत्म करो।
- धीरे खाओ।
- दौड़ो मत।
- गिर जाओगे।
- होमवर्क कर लिया?
- खिलौने समेट दो।
- टीवी बंद करो।
- अब सोने का समय है।
- अच्छे से पढ़ना।
- लड़ाई मत करो।
- शाबाश!

**Core intention**

Guide children through everyday routines using simple, natural English that sounds warm rather than commanding.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Get ready. | Casual | Preparing to leave |
| Put your shoes on. | Neutral | Getting dressed |
| Wash your hands. | Neutral | Hygiene |
| Finish your lunch. | Neutral | Mealtime |
| Be careful. | Neutral | Safety |
| Great job! | Warm | Encouragement |
| It's bedtime. | Neutral | Night routine |

---

## Nuance

English-speaking parents often use short, encouraging phrases instead of long instructions. Praise and reassurance are common.

Examples:

- Good job!
- Well done!
- Be careful.
- Take your time.

## Common Mistakes

❌ Do fast.

✅ Hurry up.

**Why?** "Hurry up" is the natural expression.

---

❌ Wear your shoes.

✅ Put your shoes on.

**Why?** The phrasal verb is more natural in everyday conversation.

---

❌ Homework finished?

✅ Have you finished your homework?

**Why?** Use a complete conversational question.
