# Thinking Pattern 023 — ATM & Everyday Banking

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Routine banking conversations are usually short and practical.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- पैसे निकालने हैं।
- बैलेंस चेक करना है।
- एटीएम काम नहीं कर रहा।
- कार्ड अंदर रह गया।
- कैश नहीं है।
- पिन याद नहीं आ रहा।
- मशीन पैसे नहीं दे रही।
- रसीद चाहिए।
- कितना निकालूँ?
- यूपीआई कर दूँ?
- बैंक कब खुलेगा?
- लाइन बहुत लंबी है।
- सर्वर डाउन है क्या?
- कार्ड ब्लॉक हो गया।
- फिर से कोशिश करता हूँ।

**Core intention**

Handle everyday banking tasks such as withdrawing cash, checking your balance, using an ATM, and making simple payments.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| I'd like to withdraw some cash. | Neutral | Cash withdrawal |
| I'd like to check my balance. | Neutral | Balance enquiry |
| The ATM isn't working. | Neutral | Machine problem |
| My card is stuck. | Neutral | ATM issue |
| Could I get a receipt, please? | Neutral | Receipt request |
| I'll try again. | Casual | Retry |

---

## Nuance

In English, people usually describe the **problem** directly:

- The ATM isn't working.
- My card is stuck.
- I can't remember my PIN.

These sound more natural than literal translations.

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ ATM is not working only.

✅ The ATM isn't working.

**Why?** Avoid unnecessary "only."

---

❌ Cash is not coming.

✅ The machine isn't dispensing cash.

**Why?** Focus on what the machine is doing.

---

❌ PIN forgotten.

✅ I can't remember my PIN.

**Why?** Use a complete sentence.

---

## Indian English vs Natural English

| Common Indian English | Natural English |
|---|---|
| Cash is not coming. | The machine isn't dispensing cash. |
| PIN forgotten. | I can't remember my PIN. |
| ATM is not working only. | The ATM isn't working. |

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** I'd like to withdraw some cash.
- **Safest:** The ATM isn't working.
- **Avoid:** "Cash is not coming."
- **Key takeaway:** Describe banking problems clearly using complete, natural sentences.
