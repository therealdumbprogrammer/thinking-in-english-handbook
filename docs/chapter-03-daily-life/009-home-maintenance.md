# Thinking Pattern 009 — Home Maintenance

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Small repairs and maintenance create frequent everyday thoughts:

- बल्ब फ्यूज़ हो गया।
- पंखा नहीं चल रहा।
- नल टपक रहा है।
- ये ठीक करना पड़ेगा।
- बैटरी खत्म हो गई।
- चार्ज लगा दो।
- स्क्रू ढीला है।
- इसे बदलना पड़ेगा।
- बिजली वाले को बुलाओ।
- प्लंबर को फोन करना है।
- अभी काम नहीं कर रहा।
- अब ठीक हो गया।

**Core intention**

Talk naturally about things that stop working, need repairing, or require routine maintenance around the house.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| The light bulb has blown. | Neutral | Bulb stopped working |
| The fan isn't working. | Neutral | Appliance problem |
| The tap is leaking. | Neutral | Plumbing |
| It needs fixing. | Neutral | General repair |
| Could you put it on charge? | Neutral | Battery low |
| We should call an electrician. | Neutral | Electrical issue |
| We need a plumber. | Neutral | Water-related issue |

---

## Nuance

Native speakers usually describe **the problem first**, then the solution.

Examples:

- The fan isn't working.
- The battery is dead.
- The tap is leaking.

Only after that do they suggest an action:

- Let's get it fixed.
- We should call an electrician.

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ The fan is not moving.

✅ The fan isn't working.

**Why?** Focus on the malfunction, not the movement.

---

❌ Current is gone.

✅ The power's out.

**Why?** "Current is gone" is Indian English.

---

❌ Battery finished.

✅ The battery is dead.

**Why?** "Dead" is the natural everyday expression.

---

## Indian English vs Natural English

| Common Indian English | Natural English |
|---|---|
| Current is gone. | The power's out. |
| Battery finished. | The battery is dead. |
| Call the electrician only. | Let's call an electrician. |

---

## Similar Expressions

| Expression | Best used when |
|---|---|
| It needs fixing. | General repair |
| It's broken. | Completely unusable |
| It's not working. | Doesn't function properly |
| We should replace it. | Replacement needed |

---

## Memory Tip

Describe **what's wrong** first, then **what should happen next**. That's the natural flow in English.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** It needs fixing.
- **Safest:** The fan isn't working.
- **Avoid:** "Current is gone" and "Battery finished."
- **Key takeaway:** State the problem clearly, then suggest the repair.
