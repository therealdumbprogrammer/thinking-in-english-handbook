# Thinking Pattern 013 — Checking on Family Members

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

In many Indian families, checking on each other is a daily habit rather than a formal inquiry.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- खाना खाया?
- पहुँच गए?
- सब ठीक है?
- कब तक आओगे?
- कहाँ पहुँचे?
- ठीक से सोए?
- तबीयत कैसी है?
- घर पहुँचकर बता देना।
- अभी कहाँ हो?
- देर तो नहीं होगी?
- सुरक्षित पहुँच जाना।
- आराम कर लिया?
- काम खत्म हो गया?
- बाहर हो क्या?
- वापस कब आओगे?

**Core intention**

Show care, confirm someone's safety, or check on their progress without sounding intrusive.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Have you eaten? | Casual | Family and close friends |
| Did you get home safely? | Neutral | After travel |
| How are you feeling? | Neutral | Health and wellbeing |
| Where are you now? | Casual | Checking location |
| What time will you be home? | Neutral | Planning |
| Let me know when you get home. | Neutral | Showing concern |

---

## Nuance

English speakers often soften these questions because the purpose is to show concern, not to monitor someone.

For example:

- *What time will you be home?* sounds more natural than *When will you come?*
- *Did you get home safely?* focuses on wellbeing rather than simply reaching home.

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ Reached?

✅ Did you get there safely?

**Why?** English normally uses a complete question.

---

❌ Have food?

✅ Have you eaten?

**Why?** Native speakers ask about the action, not the noun.

---

❌ When will you come?

✅ What time will you be home?

**Why?** This is the natural way to ask about returning home.

---

## Indian English vs Natural English

| Common Indian English | Natural English |
|---|---|
| Reached? | Did you get there safely? |
| Inform me after reaching. | Let me know when you get home. |
| Have food. | Have something to eat. / Have you eaten? |

## Memory Tip

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** Let me know when you get home.
- **Safest:** What time will you be home?
- **Avoid:** One-word questions like "Reached?"
- **Key takeaway:** Everyday check-ins in English sound caring, complete, and conversational.
