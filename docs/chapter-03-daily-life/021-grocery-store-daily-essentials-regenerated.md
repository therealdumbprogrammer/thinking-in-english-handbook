# Thinking Pattern 021 — Grocery Store (Daily Essentials)

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Buying everyday household items is one of the most common daily activities.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- दूध लेना है।
- ब्रेड खत्म हो गई।
- अंडे भी ले लेना।
- चीनी खत्म होने वाली है।
- चावल लेने हैं।
- दही भी चाहिए।
- बस यही लेना है।
- और कुछ चाहिए?
- ताज़ी सब्ज़ियाँ हैं?
- ये कितने के हैं?
- ऑफर चल रहा है?
- कार्ड चलेगा?
- बिल दे दीजिए।
- थैला है मेरे पास।
- चलो, अब घर चलते हैं।

**Core intention**

Buy daily essentials quickly and naturally while asking about availability, quantity, price, and payment.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| I'm just picking up a few things. | Neutral | Routine shopping |
| We've run out of milk. | Neutral | Need to restock |
| Do you have fresh vegetables? | Neutral | Asking about produce |
| I'll take one more. | Neutral | Adding an item |
| Can I pay by card? | Neutral | Payment |
| That's all, thank you. | Neutral | Finishing the purchase |

---

## Nuance

Everyday grocery conversations are brief. Native speakers usually mention what they need rather than describing the shopping process.

Examples:

- I'm just picking up a few things.
- We've run out of milk.
- That's all for today.

## Common Mistakes

❌ Milk has finished.

✅ We've run out of milk.

**Why?** English describes your supply, not the milk.

---

❌ Card will work?

✅ Can I pay by card?

**Why?** Ask about the payment method.

---

❌ Give one packet more.

✅ I'll take one more packet.

**Why?** This is the natural buying expression.

---

## Indian English vs Natural English

| Common Indian English | Natural English |
|---|---|
| Milk has finished. | We've run out of milk. |
| Card will work? | Can I pay by card? |
| Give one packet more. | I'll take one more packet. |

## Memory Tip

Think about **restocking the house**, not simply "buying groceries." English focuses on what you need.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** We've run out of milk.
- **Safest:** Can I pay by card?
- **Avoid:** "Milk has finished."
- **Key takeaway:** Keep grocery conversations short, practical, and focused on everyday essentials.
