# Thinking Pattern 008 — Laundry

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Laundry creates many repetitive everyday thoughts in Hindi:

- कपड़े धोने हैं।
- ये कपड़े गंदे हैं।
- मशीन चला दो।
- डिटर्जेंट खत्म हो गया।
- कपड़े सुखा देना।
- बारिश होने वाली है।
- कपड़े अंदर ले आओ।
- ये अभी गीले हैं।
- कपड़े तह कर दो।
- अलमारी में रख दो।
- ये शर्ट प्रेस करनी है।
- ये सफेद कपड़ों के साथ मत धोना।

**Core intention**

Talk naturally about washing, drying, folding, ironing and putting away clothes.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| I need to do the laundry. | Neutral | General |
| Could you start the washing machine? | Neutral | Asking for help |
| Hang these clothes out to dry. | Neutral | Drying clothes |
| These clothes are still damp. | Neutral | Not fully dry |
| Could you fold these clothes? | Neutral | Folding |
| This shirt needs ironing. | Neutral | Ironing |

---

## Nuance

English usually treats **laundry** as an activity rather than individual clothes.

Native speakers commonly say:

- I'm doing the laundry.
- I have a load of laundry.
- The laundry is done.

They rarely translate the Hindi thought word for word as "I'm washing clothes."

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ Wash the clothes machine.

✅ Start the washing machine.

**Why?** The machine is started, not washed.

---

❌ Clothes are wet only.

✅ The clothes are still wet.

**Why?** "Still" is the natural choice. Avoid using "only" for emphasis.

---

❌ Dry the clothes in sun.

✅ Hang the clothes out to dry.

**Why?** English focuses on the action rather than the location.

## Memory Tip

Think of **laundry** as one complete household activity: wash, dry, fold, iron and put away.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** I need to do the laundry.
- **Safest:** Could you hang these clothes out to dry?
- **Avoid:** Literal translations like "Wash the clothes machine."
- **Key takeaway:** Native speakers naturally use *do the laundry* to describe the whole task.
