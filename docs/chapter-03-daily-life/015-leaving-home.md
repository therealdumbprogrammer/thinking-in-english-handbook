# Thinking Pattern 015 — Leaving Home

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Leaving home involves a series of quick checks and reminders. Typical Hindi thoughts include:

- मैं निकल रहा हूँ।
- चलो, देर हो रही है।
- चाबी ले ली?
- दरवाज़ा लॉक कर देना।
- लाइट बंद कर दी?
- फोन और बटुआ रख लिया?
- छाता ले लो।
- हेलमेट पहन लो।
- गैस बंद है ना?
- पहुँचकर बता देना।
- कुछ भूल तो नहीं गए?
- मैं अभी आता हूँ।
- जल्दी वापस आऊँगा।
- चलते हैं।
- मिलते हैं शाम को।

**Core intention**

Talk naturally about preparing to leave, checking essentials, reminding others, and saying you're heading out.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| I'm heading out. | Casual | Leaving home |
| I'm off. | Casual | Short goodbye |
| Don't forget your keys. | Neutral | Reminder |
| Make sure you lock the door. | Neutral | Safety |
| I'll be back soon. | Neutral | Short outing |
| See you this evening. | Casual | Leaving family |

---

## Nuance

English speakers often announce they're leaving with short expressions:

- I'm heading out.
- I'm off.
- See you later.

They also use checklists naturally:

- Do you have your keys?
- Have you got your wallet?

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ I am going only.

✅ I'm heading out.

**Why?** Native speakers often announce departure with natural set phrases.

---

❌ Lock the door properly only.

✅ Make sure you lock the door.

**Why?** Avoid unnecessary "only".

---

❌ Take umbrella.

✅ Don't forget your umbrella.

**Why?** English often frames this as a reminder.

---

## Indian English vs Natural English

| Common Indian English | Natural English |
|---|---|
| I'm going only. | I'm heading out. |
| Lock properly. | Make sure you lock it. |
| Take helmet. | Put your helmet on. |
