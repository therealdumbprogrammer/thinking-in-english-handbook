# Thinking Pattern 022 — Pharmacy (Everyday Needs)

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Common everyday thoughts when visiting a neighbourhood pharmacy:

- दवा लेनी है।
- सिरदर्द की दवा चाहिए।
- खाँसी की दवा है?
- डॉक्टर ने ये लिखी है।
- ये दवा खत्म हो गई।
- एक और स्ट्रिप दे दीजिए।
- कितनी बार लेनी है?
- खाना खाने के बाद लेनी है?
- कोई सस्ती वाली है?
- कार्ड चलेगा?
- बिल दे दीजिए।
- धन्यवाद।

**Core intention**

Buy routine medicines and ask simple questions at a pharmacy. Detailed medical conversations belong in the Health chapter.

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| I'd like this medicine, please. | Neutral | Showing a prescription |
| Do you have this in stock? | Neutral | Availability |
| How often should I take it? | Neutral | Dosage |
| Should I take it after meals? | Neutral | Usage |
| I'll take another strip. | Neutral | Buying more |
| Could I have the bill, please? | Neutral | Checkout |

## Nuance

Keep pharmacy conversations short and practical. Focus on the medicine, availability and basic usage rather than describing medical conditions in detail.

## Real-Life Examples

**Hindi Thought:** दवा खत्म हो गई।  
**Natural English:** I've run out of this medicine.

**Hindi Thought:** ये दवा है?  
**Natural English:** Do you have this medicine?

**Hindi Thought:** खाना खाने के बाद लेनी है?  
**Natural English:** Should I take it after meals?

**Hindi Thought:** एक और स्ट्रिप दे दीजिए।  
**Natural English:** I'll take another strip.

**Hindi Thought:** बिल दे दीजिए।  
**Natural English:** Could I have the bill, please?

## Common Mistakes

❌ Medicine finished.

✅ I've run out of this medicine.

Why? English describes your supply, not the medicine itself.

❌ Give one strip.

✅ I'd like one strip, please.

Why? Requests are phrased politely.

❌ This medicine is available?

✅ Do you have this medicine?

Why? This is the natural availability question.

## Similar Expressions

| Expression | Best used when |
|---|---|
| Do you have...? | Availability |
| I'd like... | Buying |
| How often should I take it? | Instructions |
| Could I have the bill, please? | Payment |

## Memory Tip

At a pharmacy, think in terms of **buying** and **using** medicine. Keep your English polite and direct.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** Do you have this medicine?
- **Safest:** I'd like this medicine, please.
- **Avoid:** "Medicine finished."
- **Key takeaway:** Use clear, polite questions for everyday pharmacy visits.
