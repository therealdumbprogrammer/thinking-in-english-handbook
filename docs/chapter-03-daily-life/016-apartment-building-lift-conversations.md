# Thinking Pattern 016 — Apartment Building & Lift Conversations

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Apartment living creates many short, polite interactions every day.

Typical Hindi thoughts:

- कौन से फ्लोर?
- ऊपर जा रहे हैं?
- नीचे?
- पहले आप।
- लिफ्ट आ गई।
- दरवाज़ा पकड़ लीजिए।
- धन्यवाद।
- आप निकल जाइए।
- नीचे मिलते हैं।
- आज लिफ्ट बहुत धीमी है।
- भीड़ है।
- एक मिनट रुकिए।
- गुड मॉर्निंग।
- ऑफिस जा रहे हैं?
- अच्छा, फिर मिलते हैं।

**Core intention**

Handle brief, courteous conversations in lifts, corridors and apartment common areas.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Which floor? | Neutral | Inside the lift |
| Are you going up? | Neutral | Confirming direction |
| After you. | Polite | Letting someone go first |
| Hold the door, please. | Neutral | Lift or entrance |
| Thanks, I've got it. | Casual | Someone holds the door |
| See you around. | Casual | Leaving a neighbour |

---

## Nuance

These conversations are intentionally brief. Native speakers usually exchange a greeting, one practical question, and a polite closing rather than starting a long conversation.

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ Which floor you want?

✅ Which floor?

**Why?** The shorter expression is the natural choice inside a lift.

---

❌ Go first.

✅ After you.

**Why?** "After you" is the standard polite expression.

---

❌ Stop the lift.

✅ Hold the lift, please.

**Why?** You're asking someone to keep the doors open.

---

## Indian English vs Natural English

| Common Indian English | Natural English |
|---|---|
| Go first. | After you. |
| Stop the lift. | Hold the lift, please. |
| Which floor you want? | Which floor? |

## Quick Recap
