# Thinking Pattern 017 — Parking & Vehicle

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Whether driving a car or riding a two-wheeler, people think about parking, fuel, traffic, and vehicle readiness almost every day.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- गाड़ी निकालनी है।
- गाड़ी पार्क कर दो।
- पार्किंग नहीं मिल रही।
- यहाँ पार्क कर सकते हैं?
- हेलमेट पहन लो।
- सीट बेल्ट लगा लो।
- पेट्रोल कम है।
- टैंक भरवाना पड़ेगा।
- गाड़ी स्टार्ट नहीं हो रही।
- चाबी कहाँ है?
- धीरे चलाना।
- सावधानी से चलाना।
- टायर में हवा कम है।
- गाड़ी धोनी है।
- गेट खोल दो।

**Core intention**

Talk naturally about getting your vehicle ready, parking it safely, and dealing with common day-to-day driving situations.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| I'm taking the car. | Neutral | Leaving home |
| I can't find a parking space. | Neutral | Looking for parking |
| Can I park here? | Neutral | Asking about parking |
| Put your seat belt on. | Neutral | Before driving |
| Don't forget your helmet. | Neutral | Two-wheeler |
| The fuel tank is almost empty. | Neutral | Low fuel |
| The car won't start. | Neutral | Vehicle problem |

---

## Nuance

Native speakers usually describe **the situation**, not the process.

Instead of translating:

> पार्किंग नहीं मिल रही।

they naturally say:

> I can't find a parking space.

Similarly, instead of saying:

> Petrol is less.

they say:

> I'm running low on fuel.

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ Parking is not getting.

✅ I can't find a parking space.

**Why?** English focuses on the person's situation, not on "parking getting."

---

❌ Petrol is less.

✅ I'm running low on fuel.

**Why?** This is the natural everyday expression.

---

❌ Wear helmet.

✅ Put your helmet on.

**Why?** "Put on" is the natural phrasal verb.

## Memory Tip

Think about **the situation you're facing**, not the literal Hindi wording. English naturally describes the problem first, then the action.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** I can't find a parking space.
- **Safest:** Can I park here?
- **Avoid:** "Parking is not getting."
- **Key takeaway:** Describe the situation naturally instead of translating Hindi word for word.
