# Thinking Pattern 018 — Talking to Neighbours

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Neighbours are part of everyday life. These conversations are usually short, friendly and practical.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- नमस्ते, कैसे हैं?
- सुबह की सैर पर जा रहे हैं?
- काफी दिनों बाद मिले।
- सब ठीक है?
- घर पर सब कैसे हैं?
- बाहर जा रहे हैं?
- आज मौसम अच्छा है।
- कुछ चाहिए तो बताइए।
- कल नहीं दिखे।
- शाम को मिलते हैं।
- बच्चे कैसे हैं?
- छुट्टी पर जा रहे हैं?
- कोई दिक्कत तो नहीं?
- फिर मिलते हैं।

**Core intention**

Build friendly relationships through brief everyday conversations without becoming overly formal.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Hi, how's it going? | Casual | Passing by |
| Good morning! | Neutral | Morning greeting |
| Haven't seen you in a while. | Neutral | After some time |
| How's the family? | Neutral | Familiar neighbours |
| Beautiful weather today, isn't it? | Neutral | Casual small talk |
| See you around. | Casual | Ending the conversation |

---

## Nuance

Neighbour conversations in English are usually light and positive. They rarely become long unless both people want to continue talking. A greeting, one or two questions, and a friendly goodbye are common.

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ Where are you going?

✅ Heading somewhere?

**Why?** A softer question sounds friendlier in casual encounters.

---

❌ All well?

✅ Is everything going well?

**Why?** Use a complete conversational question.

---

❌ We will meet.

✅ See you around.

**Why?** This is the natural casual farewell.

---

## Indian English vs Natural English

| Common Indian English | Natural English |
|---|---|
| All well? | Is everything going well? |
| We will meet. | See you around. |
| Where going? | Heading somewhere? |

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** Haven't seen you in a while.
- **Safest:** Hi, how's it going?
- **Avoid:** Literal translations like "We will meet."
- **Key takeaway:** Friendly neighbour conversations are brief, positive and natural.
