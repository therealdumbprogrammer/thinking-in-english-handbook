# Thinking Pattern 034 — Leaving After a Visit

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

When a visit comes to an end, people naturally signal that it's time to leave, thank the host, and make polite closing remarks.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- अब चलते हैं।
- बहुत अच्छा लगा।
- काफी देर हो गई।
- फिर मिलेंगे।
- आने के लिए धन्यवाद।
- आपको तकलीफ़ दी।
- घर पहुँचकर बता दूँगा।
- कभी आप भी आइए।
- समय कैसे निकल गया पता ही नहीं चला।
- अब निकलना चाहिए।
- चलता हूँ।
- ध्यान से जाइए।
- फिर मिलते हैं।
- शुभ रात्रि।
- अपना ख्याल रखिए।

**Core intention**

End a visit politely while expressing appreciation and leaving on a warm note.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| We'd better get going. | Neutral | It's time to leave |
| We should head out. | Casual | Leaving |
| Thanks for having us. | Neutral | Thanking the host |
| We had a great time. | Friendly | Appreciating the visit |
| Take care. | Casual | Goodbye |
| See you again soon. | Friendly | Ending the visit |

---

## Nuance

Native speakers usually avoid leaving abruptly. They often signal their departure first, thank the host, then say goodbye.

---

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ I am leaving now only.

✅ We'd better get going.

**Why?** This sounds more natural and conversational.

❌ Thanks for invitation.

✅ Thanks for having us.

**Why?** Native speakers thank the host for the experience.

❌ We will meet.

✅ See you again soon.

**Why?** This is the natural farewell.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** Thanks for having us.
- **Safest:** We'd better get going.
- **Avoid:** "We will meet."
- **Key takeaway:** End visits with appreciation, then a warm farewell.
