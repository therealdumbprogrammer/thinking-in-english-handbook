# Thinking Pattern 029 — Finding & Locating Things

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Everyday life is full of moments when we look for things or ask where something is.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- मेरी चाबी कहाँ है?
- फोन देखा क्या?
- रिमोट नहीं मिल रहा।
- चश्मा कहाँ रखा?
- यहीं तो रखा था।
- मिल गया!
- अभी यहीं था।
- शायद बैग में हो।
- ज़रा देखना।
- अलमारी में देखो।
- कहीं गिर तो नहीं गया?
- किसने उठाया?
- याद नहीं कहाँ रखा।
- फिर से ढूँढते हैं।
- मिल ही नहीं रहा।

**Core intention**

Talk naturally about searching for everyday items, asking others for help, and describing whether something has been found.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Have you seen my keys? | Neutral | Looking for an item |
| I can't find my phone. | Neutral | Unable to locate something |
| It was here a minute ago. | Casual | Item was recently seen |
| Could you help me look for it? | Neutral | Asking for help |
| I found it. | Casual | Item located |
| It must be somewhere around here. | Neutral | Guessing the location |

---

## Nuance

English speakers usually describe the **search** rather than the missing object.

For example:

- I can't find my wallet.
- Have you seen my glasses?
- It was here a minute ago.

These sound much more natural than literal translations such as *My wallet is not getting.*

## Common Mistakes

❌ My keys are not getting.

✅ I can't find my keys.

**Why?** English describes your search, not whether the item is "getting."

---

❌ Where I kept it?

✅ Where did I put it?

**Why?** Use the past tense naturally.

---

❌ It was here only.

✅ It was here a minute ago.

**Why?** Avoid using "only" for emphasis.

---

## Indian English vs Natural English

| Common Indian English | Natural English |
|---|---|
| My phone is not getting. | I can't find my phone. |
| Where I kept it? | Where did I put it? |
| It was here only. | It was here a minute ago. |

---

## Similar Expressions

| Expression | Best used when |
|---|---|
| Have you seen...? | Asking someone |
| I can't find... | Searching |
| I found it. | Search complete |
| It must be around here. | Guessing |

---

## Memory Tip

When something is missing, think **I'm searching for it**, not **it's missing**. English naturally focuses on your action.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** I can't find my...
- **Safest:** Have you seen...?
- **Avoid:** "My keys are not getting."
- **Key takeaway:** Describe the search, then ask for help if needed.
