# Thinking Pattern 025 — Security Guard Interactions

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

In apartments, offices and residential societies, brief conversations with security guards happen almost every day.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- गेट खोल दीजिए।
- पार्सल आया है?
- कैब आ गई?
- मेरा मेहमान आएगा।
- मैं अभी आता हूँ।
- ये मेरे लिए है।
- किस फ्लैट में जाना है?
- विज़िटर एंट्री करनी है।
- गाड़ी अंदर ले जानी है।
- डिलीवरी वाले को भेज दीजिए।
- धन्यवाद।
- शुभ रात्रि।

**Core intention**

Handle short, practical conversations with security staff politely and efficiently.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Could you open the gate, please? | Neutral | Entering or leaving |
| Has my parcel arrived? | Neutral | Parcel enquiry |
| Has my cab arrived? | Neutral | Waiting for transport |
| I'm expecting a guest. | Neutral | Informing security |
| Please send the delivery person up. | Neutral | Deliveries |
| Thank you. Have a good day. | Polite | Ending interaction |

---

## Nuance

These conversations are usually brief and task-focused. Native speakers use polite requests such as *could you* and *please* rather than direct commands.

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ Open the gate.

✅ Could you open the gate, please?

**Why?** A polite request sounds more natural.

---

❌ Parcel came?

✅ Has my parcel arrived?

**Why?** Use the present perfect for recent events.

---

❌ Guest is coming only.

✅ I'm expecting a guest.

**Why?** This is the idiomatic expression.

---

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** I'm expecting a guest.
- **Safest:** Could you open the gate, please?
- **Avoid:** Literal questions like "Parcel came?"
- **Key takeaway:** Use polite, complete sentences for everyday security interactions.
