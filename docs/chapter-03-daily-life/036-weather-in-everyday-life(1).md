# Thinking Pattern 036 — Weather in Everyday Life

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Weather influences everyday decisions at home, while commuting, and during outdoor activities.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- आज बहुत गर्मी है।
- आज बहुत ठंड है।
- बारिश होने वाली है।
- लगता है बारिश आ जाएगी।
- छाता ले लो।
- धूप बहुत तेज़ है।
- हवा तेज़ चल रही है।
- बाहर मत निकलना।
- कपड़े अंदर ले आओ।
- मौसम अच्छा हो गया।
- शाम तक बारिश हो सकती है।
- आज उमस बहुत है।
- खिड़की बंद कर दो।
- एसी चला दो।
- मौसम अचानक बदल गया।

**Core intention**

Talk naturally about the weather and explain how it affects everyday plans and routines.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| It's really hot today. | Neutral | Hot weather |
| It's freezing outside. | Casual | Very cold weather |
| It looks like it's going to rain. | Neutral | Predicting rain |
| Don't forget your umbrella. | Neutral | Reminder |
| It's quite windy today. | Neutral | Windy weather |
| The weather's lovely today. | Casual | Pleasant weather |

---

## Nuance

English speakers often use the weather as part of everyday conversation. Rather than naming the weather, they usually describe how it feels or what is likely to happen.

Examples:

- It's really hot today.
- It's freezing outside.
- It looks like it's going to rain.
- What a beautiful day!

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ Heat is too much.

✅ It's really hot today.

**Why?** English describes the weather rather than the amount of heat.

---

❌ Rain is coming.

✅ It looks like it's going to rain.

**Why?** Native speakers naturally describe a prediction this way.

---

❌ Climate is good.

✅ The weather's nice today.

**Why?** *Weather* refers to day-to-day conditions; *climate* refers to long-term patterns.

## Memory Tip

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** It looks like it's going to rain.
- **Safest:** It's really hot today.
- **Avoid:** "Rain is coming."
- **Key takeaway:** Connect the weather to everyday actions and decisions.
