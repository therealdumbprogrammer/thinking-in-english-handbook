# Thinking Pattern 012 — Reminding Family Members

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

In daily life, we constantly remind family members about small but important things.

Typical Hindi thoughts include:

- भूलना मत।
- याद से कर लेना।
- याद दिला देना।
- निकलने से पहले बता देना।
- ताला लगा लेना।
- दूध लेते आना।
- फोन चार्ज कर लेना।
- दवा खा लेना।
- समय पर निकलना।
- बच्चों को ले आना।
- गैस बंद कर देना।
- लाइट बंद करके जाना।
- छाता ले जाना।
- पहुँचकर बता देना।
- अलार्म लगा लेना।

**Core intention**

Help someone remember an action before they forget it.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Don't forget to... | Neutral | Everyday reminders |
| Make sure you... | Neutral | Important reminders |
| Remember to... | Neutral | Gentle reminder |
| Let me know when you get there. | Neutral | Travel/home |
| Be sure to lock the door. | Neutral | Home safety |
| Could you remind me later? | Neutral | Asking for a reminder |

---

## Nuance

English reminders usually focus on the **future action**, not the possibility of forgetting.

Instead of translating:

> भूलना मत।

Native speakers naturally say:

- Don't forget to...
- Remember to...
- Make sure you...

These sound supportive rather than commanding.

## Real-Life Examples

### Leaving Home

**Hindi Thought**

दूध लेते आना।

**Natural English**

> Don't forget to pick up some milk.

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ Don't forget milk.

✅ Don't forget to buy some milk.

**Why?** English naturally mentions the action.

---

❌ Inform after reaching.

✅ Let me know when you get there.

**Why?** This is the idiomatic expression.

---

❌ Take medicine timely.

✅ Make sure you take your medicine on time.

**Why?** Use a complete reminder pattern.

---

## Indian English vs Natural English

| Common Indian English | Natural English |
|---|---|
| Inform me after reaching. | Let me know when you get there. |
| Don't forget milk. | Don't forget to buy some milk. |
| Take medicine timely. | Take your medicine on time. |

---

## Similar Expressions

| Expression | Best used when |
|---|---|
| Don't forget to... | Everyday reminders |
| Remember to... | Friendly reminders |
| Make sure you... | Important reminders |
| Be sure to... | Slightly stronger reminder |

---

## Memory Tip

Think about **helping someone remember**, not warning them. English reminders are usually calm and supportive.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** Don't forget to...
- **Safest:** Remember to...
- **Avoid:** Literal translations like "Inform after reaching."
- **Key takeaway:** Focus on the action the person should remember.
