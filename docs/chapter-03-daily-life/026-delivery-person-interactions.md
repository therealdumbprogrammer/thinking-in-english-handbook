# Thinking Pattern 026 — Delivery Person Interactions

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Receiving deliveries has become part of everyday life.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- मेरा ऑर्डर आया?
- नीचे आ रहा हूँ।
- ऊपर आ जाइए।
- दरवाज़े पर छोड़ दीजिए।
- ओटीपी बताना है।
- ऑनलाइन पेमेंट हो चुका है।
- कैश दूँ?
- थोड़ा इंतज़ार कीजिए।
- सही पता मिल गया?
- एक मिनट।
- धन्यवाद।
- अच्छा दिन हो।

**Core intention**

Receive parcels and food deliveries politely while coordinating location, payment and handover.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| I'm coming down. | Casual | Going downstairs |
| Could you come up, please? | Neutral | Apartment delivery |
| Please leave it at the door. | Neutral | Contactless delivery |
| The payment has already been made. | Neutral | Online payment |
| Here's the OTP. | Neutral | Verification |
| Thanks. Have a good day. | Polite | Ending interaction |

---

## Nuance

Keep delivery conversations short and practical. Native speakers usually exchange only the information needed to complete the delivery.

## Real-Life Examples

**Hindi Thought:** नीचे आ रहा हूँ।  
**Natural English:** I'm coming down.

**Hindi Thought:** दरवाज़े पर छोड़ दीजिए।  
**Natural English:** Please leave it at the door.

**Hindi Thought:** ऑनलाइन पेमेंट हो चुका है।  
**Natural English:** It's already been paid.

**Hindi Thought:** ओटीपी ये है।  
**Natural English:** Here's the OTP.

**Hindi Thought:** एक मिनट रुकिए।  
**Natural English:** Just a moment, please.

## Common Mistakes

❌ I am coming in two minutes only.

✅ I'll be there in two minutes.

Why? Avoid unnecessary "only".

❌ Leave outside.

✅ Please leave it at the door.

Why? Be specific about the location.

❌ Payment done.

✅ The payment has already been made.

Why? Use a complete sentence.

## Similar Expressions

| Expression | Best used when |
|---|---|
| I'm coming down. | Apartment pickup |
| Please leave it at the door. | Contactless delivery |
| It's already been paid. | Online payment |
| Just a moment, please. | Short wait |

## Memory Tip

Focus on **location**, **payment** and **handover**. Most delivery conversations need only these three things.
