# Thinking Pattern 007 — Cleaning

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Cleaning is a daily activity that generates many short, practical thoughts. Hindi speakers commonly think:

- सफाई करनी है।
- कमरा बिखरा हुआ है।
- झाड़ू लगा दो।
- पोछा लगा दिया?
- धूल साफ कर दो।
- मेज़ साफ कर दो।
- कचरा बाहर फेंक दो।
- बाथरूम साफ करना है।
- पहले ये साफ कर लेते हैं।
- बाद में कर लेंगे।
- सब कुछ अपनी जगह रखो।
- कमरा अब साफ लग रहा है।

**Core intention**

Coordinate everyday cleaning tasks, ask for help, and describe cleanliness naturally.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Let's clean up. | Casual | Starting together |
| Could you sweep the floor? | Neutral | Asking for help |
| I'll mop the floor. | Neutral | Taking responsibility |
| Please take out the trash. | Neutral | Household chore |
| Wipe the table, please. | Neutral | Cleaning surfaces |
| The room looks much better now. | Casual | After cleaning |

---

## Nuance

Native speakers often use **clean up** for general tidying and **clean** for the activity itself.

Examples:

- Let's clean up before the guests arrive.
- I'm cleaning the bathroom.
- Could you tidy up your room?

"Tidy up" usually means putting things back in their place, while "clean" often involves removing dirt.

## Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ Do cleaning.

✅ Clean the house.

**Why?** Native speakers normally use the verb **clean** directly.

---

❌ Throw the garbage outside.

✅ Take out the trash.

**Why?** "Take out the trash" is the standard everyday expression.

---

❌ Room is dirty only.

✅ The room is still dirty.

**Why?** Avoid using "only" for emphasis.

## Memory Tip

Think about **making a place clean**, not about translating "सफाई करना". English usually uses a simple verb: **clean**, **tidy up**, **wipe**, **sweep**, or **mop**.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** Let's clean up.
- **Safest:** Could you sweep the floor?
- **Avoid:** "Do cleaning."
- **Key takeaway:** Use specific action verbs like *clean*, *tidy up*, *sweep*, *mop*, and *wipe*.
