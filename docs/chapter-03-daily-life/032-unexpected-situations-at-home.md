# Thinking Pattern 032 — Unexpected Situations at Home

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Daily life rarely goes exactly as planned. Small surprises and inconveniences happen at home all the time.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- अरे, ये क्या हो गया?
- कुछ गिर गया।
- ये टूट गया।
- पानी फैल गया।
- बिजली चली गई।
- गैस खत्म हो गई।
- दरवाज़ा अटक गया।
- चाबी अंदर रह गई।
- फोन गिर गया।
- ये कैसे हुआ?
- कोई बात नहीं।
- संभाल लेंगे।
- पहले इसे साफ करते हैं।
- चिंता मत करो।
- अब क्या करें?

**Core intention**

React naturally to small unexpected situations, reassure others, and decide what to do next.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Oh no! | Casual | Immediate reaction |
| What happened? | Neutral | Asking what went wrong |
| It's okay. | Neutral | Reassuring someone |
| Don't worry about it. | Neutral | Comforting |
| Let's sort it out. | Neutral | Solving the problem together |
| We'll figure something out. | Neutral | When no immediate solution exists |

---

## Nuance

Native speakers often react briefly, then move quickly to a solution.

- Oh no!
- It's okay.
- Let's clean this up.
- We'll sort it out.

The focus is usually on resolving the problem rather than blaming someone.

---

## Common Mistakes

❌ Glass is broken only.

✅ The glass broke.

**Why?** Describe the event naturally without adding "only".

❌ Water fell.

✅ I spilled some water.

**Why?** Liquids are usually *spilled*.

❌ Tension mat lo.

✅ Don't worry about it.

**Why?** This is the natural reassurance.

---

## Indian English vs Natural English

| Common Indian English | Natural English |
|---|---|
| Water fell. | I spilled some water. |
| Glass is broken only. | The glass broke. |
| No tension. | Don't worry about it. |

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** Don't worry about it.
- **Safest:** Let's sort it out.
- **Avoid:** Literal expressions like "Water fell."
- **Key takeaway:** Natural English focuses on reassurance and solving the problem.
