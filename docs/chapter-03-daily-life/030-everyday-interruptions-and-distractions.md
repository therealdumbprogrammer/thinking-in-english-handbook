# Thinking Pattern 030 — Everyday Interruptions & Distractions

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Daily life is full of interruptions that require quick, natural responses.

Representative Hindi thoughts:

- एक मिनट।
- ज़रा रुको।
- अभी व्यस्त हूँ।
- बाद में बात करते हैं।
- फोन आ रहा है।
- कोई दरवाज़े पर है।
- बीच में मत बोलो।
- मेरी बात पूरी होने दो।
- ध्यान भटक गया।
- मैं भूल गया कि क्या कह रहा था।
- एक काम निपटा लूँ।
- अभी नहीं हो पाएगा।
- फिर से बताना।
- कहाँ थे हम?
- चलो, अब जारी रखते हैं।

**Core intention**

Pause, resume, or redirect a conversation politely when something interrupts you.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Just a second. | Casual | Brief interruption |
| Hang on a moment. | Casual | Asking someone to wait |
| I'm in the middle of something. | Neutral | Currently busy |
| Can we come back to this later? | Neutral | Postponing |
| Sorry, where were we? | Neutral | Resuming a conversation |
| I lost my train of thought. | Neutral | After being distracted |

---

## Nuance

English speakers usually acknowledge the interruption before returning to the conversation.

Examples:

- Just a second.
- Sorry about that.
- Where were we?

This keeps the conversation smooth and polite.

## Common Mistakes

❌ Wait only.

✅ Just a second.

**Why?** English uses reassuring expressions rather than commands.

---

❌ I am busy now only.

✅ I'm in the middle of something.

**Why?** This sounds more natural and specific.

---

❌ What I was saying?

✅ What was I saying?

**Why?** Use natural question word order.

---

## Indian English vs Natural English

| Common Indian English | Natural English |
|---|---|
| Wait only. | Just a second. |
| I am busy now only. | I'm in the middle of something. |
| What I was saying? | What was I saying? |

## Similar Expressions

| Expression | Best used when |
|---|---|
| Just a second. | Very short interruption |
| Hang on a moment. | Asking someone to wait |
| Can we come back to this later? | Delaying discussion |
| Sorry, where were we? | Resuming |

---

## Memory Tip

When interrupted, **acknowledge**, **pause**, then **resume**. English conversations flow more smoothly when you guide the listener through the interruption.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** Just a second.
- **Safest:** I'm in the middle of something.
- **Avoid:** "Wait only."
- **Key takeaway:** Use polite transition phrases to handle interruptions naturally.
