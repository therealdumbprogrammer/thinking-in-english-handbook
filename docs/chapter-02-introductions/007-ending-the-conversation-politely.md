# Thinking Pattern 007 — Ending the Conversation Politely

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

As a conversation comes to a natural end, Hindi speakers often think:

- अब चलता हूँ।
- अब निकलना चाहिए।
- अच्छा लगा आपसे मिलकर।
- फिर मिलते हैं।
- अब मुझे जाना होगा।
- देर हो रही है।
- आपको रोकना नहीं चाहता।
- अब आपकी छुट्टी।
- उम्मीद है फिर मुलाकात होगी।
- बातचीत अच्छी लगी।

**Core intention**

End the conversation in a warm, natural way without sounding abrupt or making the other person feel dismissed.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|------------|----------|-------------|
| It was nice talking to you. | Neutral | Almost any conversation |
| It was great meeting you. | Neutral | First meetings |
| I'd better get going. | Casual | Leaving naturally |
| I'll let you get back to it. | Professional | When someone is busy |
| Enjoy the rest of your day. | Neutral | Friendly closing |
| Have a safe trip. | Neutral | Travel |
| See you around. | Casual | Friends, colleagues |
| Take care. | Neutral | Everyday conversations |

---

## Nuance

Native speakers usually give a small reason before leaving.

Instead of suddenly saying "Bye", they often soften the ending.

Examples:

- I'd better get going.
- I should let you get back to work.
- I won't keep you any longer.

This makes the conversation feel complete instead of interrupted.

## Common Thinking Traps

❌ Bye only.

✅ Bye! Take care.

**Why?**

Adding a short closing phrase sounds warmer and more natural.

---

❌ We will meet.

✅ See you around.

**Why?**

English usually uses a conversational farewell rather than a literal future statement.

---

❌ I am going.

✅ I'd better get going.

**Why?**

Native speakers often soften the departure instead of announcing it directly.

## Memory Tip

Don't end a conversation suddenly.

Give a short closing remark, wish the other person well, and then leave.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** It was nice talking to you.
- **Safest:** Take care.
- **Avoid:** Literal endings like "We will meet."
- **Key takeaway:** A polite ending leaves a positive final impression.
