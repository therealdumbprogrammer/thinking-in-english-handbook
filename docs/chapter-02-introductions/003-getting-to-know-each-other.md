# Thinking Pattern 003 — Getting to Know Each Other

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

Once the conversation has started, Hindi speakers naturally think:

- आपका नाम क्या है?
- आप कहाँ से हैं?
- आप क्या करते हैं?
- यहाँ कैसे आना हुआ?
- क्या आप भी पहली बार आए हैं?
- किस कंपनी में हैं?
- क्या आप यहीं रहते हैं?
- पढ़ाई कर रहे हैं या काम?
- आपके बारे में थोड़ा जानूँ।
- बातचीत आगे बढ़ाते हैं।

**Core intention**

The goal is to know the other person a little better while sharing basic information about yourself.

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|

| What do you do? | Neutral | Everyday conversations |
| Where are you from? | Neutral | First meetings |
| What brings you here? | Neutral | Events, travel |
| Are you from around here? | Casual | Local conversations |
| What do you do for work? | Professional | Networking |

---

## Nuance

English conversations usually alternate between asking and sharing.

Instead of asking five questions in a row, native speakers often answer their own question after the other person responds.

Example:

> A: Where are you from?
>
> B: Pune.
>
> A: Nice! I'm from Delhi.

That keeps the conversation balanced.

## Common Thinking Traps

❌ What is your good name?

✅ What's your name?

**Why?** "Good name" is an Indian English expression and is uncommon in natural spoken English.

---

❌ From where are you?

✅ Where are you from?

**Why?** Native speakers naturally place the question word first.

---

❌ Which company you are working?

✅ Which company do you work for?

**Why?** English questions require the helping verb and the preposition "for" in this pattern.

## Similar Expressions

| Expression | Best used when |
|---|---|
| What's your name? | Any first meeting |

| What brings you here? | Events, travel, conferences |

## Memory Tip

Ask one question, listen to the answer, then share something about yourself. Good conversations feel like a game of catch, not an interview.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** What do you do?
- **Safest:** Where are you from?
- **Avoid:** "What is your good name?" and literal Hindi word order.
- **Key takeaway:** Balance curiosity with sharing about yourself.
