# Thinking Pattern 009 — High-Frequency Hindi Thoughts During First Conversations

## Why this Pattern Exists

This chapter already covers the major thinking patterns of introductions and small talk.

This supplementary pattern captures dozens of **high-frequency Hindi thoughts** that naturally occur during first meetings but don't justify separate thinking patterns.

Use it as a quick-reference companion.

---

## Starting the Interaction

| Hindi Thought | Natural English |
|---|---|
| नमस्ते बोल दूँ। | I'll say hi. |
| हेलो बोल देता हूँ। | Hi! |
| एक्सक्यूज़ मी। | Excuse me. |
| आपको डिस्टर्ब तो नहीं कर रहा? | Am I catching you at a bad time? |
| दो मिनट मिलेंगे? | Do you have a minute? |
| क्या अभी बात कर सकते हैं? | Is now a good time? |

---

## Breaking the Ice

| Hindi Thought | Natural English |
|---|---|
| बहुत भीड़ है। | It's quite busy today. |
| काफी ट्रैफिक था। | Traffic was terrible today. |
| यहाँ पहली बार आए हैं? | Is this your first time here? |
| रास्ता आसानी से मिल गया? | Did you find the place easily? |
| लाइन काफी लंबी है। | That's quite a long line. |
| मौसम अच्छा है। | Nice weather today. |

---

## Getting to Know Someone

| Hindi Thought | Natural English |
|---|---|
| आपका नाम क्या है? | What's your name? |
| आप कहाँ से हैं? | Where are you from? |
| क्या करते हैं? | What do you do? |
| किस कंपनी में हैं? | Which company do you work for? |
| यहीं रहते हैं? | Do you live nearby? |
| यहाँ कैसे आना हुआ? | What brings you here? |

---

## Keeping the Conversation Going

| Hindi Thought | Natural English |
|---|---|
| अच्छा? | Oh, really? |
| फिर? | And then? / What happened next? |
| सच में? | Really? |
| उसके बाद? | What happened after that? |
| वाह! | That's great! |
| कमाल है। | That's amazing! |
| और बताइए। | Tell me more. |

---

## Building a Connection

| Hindi Thought | Natural English |
|---|---|
| मुझे भी। | Me too. |
| मेरे साथ भी ऐसा हुआ। | The same thing happened to me. |
| बिल्कुल। | Absolutely. |
| मेरी भी यही राय है। | I feel the same way. |
| मुझे भी पसंद है। | I like it too. |
| हम दोनों एक ही शहर के हैं। | We're from the same city. |

---

## Ending the Conversation

| Hindi Thought | Natural English |
|---|---|
| अब चलता हूँ। | I'd better get going. |
| अच्छा लगा आपसे मिलकर। | It was great meeting you. |
| फिर मिलते हैं। | See you around. |
| ध्यान रखिए। | Take care. |
| घर पहुँचकर बताइए। | Let me know when you get home. |

## Staying Connected

| Hindi Thought | Natural English |
|---|---|
| संपर्क में रहेंगे। | Let's keep in touch. |
| नंबर सेव कर लीजिए। | Save my number. |
| मुझे मैसेज कर देना। | Drop me a message. |
| LinkedIn पर जुड़ते हैं। | Let's connect on LinkedIn. |
| कभी कॉफी पर मिलते हैं। | Let's grab a coffee sometime. |

---

## Common Indian English vs Natural English

| Common Indian English | Natural English |
|---|---|
| What is your good name? | What's your name? |
| I belong to Delhi. | I'm from Delhi. |
| I am doing a job. | I work as... / I have a job. |
| Can we do friendship? | Let's keep in touch. |
| Where do you do job? | Where do you work? |

---

## Memory Tip

Don't memorize every sentence.

Recognize the **Hindi thought**, identify the **communication goal**, then choose the English expression that feels most natural for the situation.
