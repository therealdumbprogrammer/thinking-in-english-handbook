# Thinking Pattern 008 — Staying Connected

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

When a conversation has gone well, Hindi speakers often think:

- इनसे फिर बात करनी चाहिए।
- नंबर ले लेता हूँ।
- संपर्क में रहेंगे।
- LinkedIn पर जुड़ जाते हैं।
- कभी मिलते हैं।
- आपका नंबर मिल सकता है?
- संपर्क बना रहता है।
- आगे भी बात करेंगे।
- फिर मिलते हैं।
- अच्छा लगा, जुड़े रहते हैं।

**Core intention**

The conversation is ending, but you would like the relationship to continue in the future.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Let's keep in touch. | Neutral | Friends and colleagues |
| Here's my number. | Casual | After a good conversation |
| Are you on LinkedIn? | Professional | Networking events |
| I'd love to stay in touch. | Neutral | Professional and social |
| Feel free to reach out. | Professional | Business conversations |
| Let's catch up sometime. | Casual | Existing acquaintances |
| It would be great to connect. | Professional | Conferences |

---

## Nuance

Native speakers usually make the next step specific.

Instead of saying only "We'll stay in touch," they often suggest *how*:

- Connect on LinkedIn
- Exchange numbers
- Share an email
- Plan to catch up

A concrete action makes the invitation feel genuine.

## Common Thinking Traps

❌ We will always be in contact.

✅ Let's keep in touch.

**Why?**

English prefers simple, natural expressions.

---

❌ Give me your contact.

✅ Could I have your number?

**Why?**

A polite request sounds much more natural.

---

❌ Message me compulsory.

✅ Drop me a message sometime.

**Why?**

Avoid direct commands when making friendly invitations.

## Memory Tip

If you genuinely want to stay connected, suggest one simple next step instead of making a vague promise.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** Let's keep in touch.
- **Safest:** Are you on LinkedIn?
- **Avoid:** Literal translations like "Give me your contact."
- **Key takeaway:** End the conversation with a clear and natural way to continue the relationship.
