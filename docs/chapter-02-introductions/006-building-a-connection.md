# Thinking Pattern 006 — Building a Connection

## Hindi Thinking Pattern

As the conversation becomes comfortable, Hindi speakers often think:

- अरे, ये तो मेरी तरह हैं।
- हमारी तो काफी बातें मिलती हैं।
- मुझे भी यही पसंद है।
- हम दोनों एक ही शहर के हैं।
- हमारी फील्ड भी एक जैसी है।
- इनसे फिर बात करनी चाहिए।
- अच्छी बातचीत हो रही है।
- इनसे जुड़ाव महसूस हो रहा है.
- हम दोनों का अनुभव मिलता-जुलता है।
- ये तो मज़ेदार संयोग है।

**Core intention**

Move beyond exchanging information and create a genuine personal connection.

---

## Natural English Expressions

| Expression | Register | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Me too! | Casual | Shared interests |
| Same here. | Casual | Similar opinions or experiences |
| We have that in common. | Neutral | Shared background |
| I know exactly what you mean. | Neutral | Shared experience |
| I've always wanted to try that. | Neutral | Showing interest |
| That's something we both enjoy. | Professional | Networking |

---

## Nuance

People usually connect over similarities, not impressive stories. Even a small shared experience can make a conversation warmer.

Rather than changing the subject, acknowledge the connection before moving on.

## Common Thinking Traps

❌ We both are same.

✅ We're quite similar.

**Why?** Native speakers describe the similarity, not "same" as a noun.

---

❌ Same pinch!

✅ Me too! / Same here.

**Why?** "Same pinch" is not common in everyday native English.

---

❌ Our thinking matches.

✅ We think alike.

**Why?** This is the natural idiomatic expression.

---

## Similar Expressions

| Expression | Best used when |
|---|---|
| Me too! | Shared actions or likes |
| Same here. | Shared opinions |
| We have that in common. | Shared background |
| I know exactly what you mean. | Shared experiences |

---

## Memory Tip

Connection grows when you notice similarities. Respond to what you share before introducing a new topic.

## Quick Recap

- **Most useful:** Me too!

- **Avoid:** Literal expressions like "Same pinch."
- **Key takeaway:** Shared experiences build rapport naturally.
